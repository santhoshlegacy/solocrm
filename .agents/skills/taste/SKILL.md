# Design Taste Skill

1. **Art Direction & Personality**: Design should look crafted by a senior human designer, not generated by an LLM template.
2. **Organic Sophistication**: Warm palettes, editorial proportions, restrained accent usage, and asymmetric visual rhythm.
3. **Restraint over Gimmick**: Accents must be used sparingly for high-value signals rather than splattered everywhere.
4. **Natural Light & Glassmorphism**: When using frosted glass, ensure specular rim lighting, subtle translucent boundaries, and organic background refraction.
