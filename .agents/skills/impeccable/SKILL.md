# Impeccable Frontend Design Auditor

1. **Pixel-Perfect Alignment & Contrast**: Verify AA/AAA accessibility contrast between text and backgrounds.
2. **Glassmorphism Structural Integrity**: Ensure backdrop filters, inset specular highlights, and borders do not clip or break during hover transitions or responsive reflows.
3. **Motion Fluidity**: Check that Framer Motion `layoutId` pills, page transitions (`scale: 0.98` to `1`), and staggered card entrances do not stutter or cause layout shifts (CLS).
4. **Data Density & Ergonomics**: Clean data presentation with tabular figures, distinct badges, and ergonomic touch/click targets.
